# Feature Suggestions & Integrations

## Core ERP Features
1. **Inventory Management**: Track stock levels, low-stock alerts, purchase orders generation to suppliers, and goods receipt notes.
2. **HR & Payroll**: Manage employee files, attendance, leave requests, and payroll generation.
3. **Advanced Accounting**: Double-entry ledger, Chart of Accounts, Profit & Loss statements, Balance Sheet, Journal Entries, and Bank Reconciliation.
4. **CRM Capabilities**: Lead tracking, sales funnel stages (kanban board expansion), activity logging, and email marketing.
5. **Project Management**: Expand BOQ/BOM into full project execution with Gantt charts, milestones, resource allocation, and timesheets.

## AI Improvements
1. **Document Parsing (OCR)**: Upload supplier invoices or RFQs and use AI to automatically populate BOQ, products, and invoices.
2. **Predictive Analytics**: Sales forecasting based on historical CRM and Quotation data.
3. **Automated Follow-ups**: AI drafting follow-up emails for overdue invoices or expiring quotations.
4. **Smart Categorization**: Automatically categorizing expenses or products into appropriate accounting buckets.

## 3rd-Party Integrations
1. **Payment Gateways**: Stripe, PayPal, or regional gateways (e.g., PayTabs, Moyasar in MENA) for direct invoice payment links.
2. **Accounting Software Sync**: Integration with Xero, QuickBooks, or Zoho Books.
3. **Communication Tools**: Twilio for SMS alerts, WhatsApp Business API for instant client notifications, Slack/Teams for internal updates.
4. **ZATCA Phase 2 Integration (Saudi Arabia)**: Since Phase 1 ZATCA QR is implemented, API integration for Phase 2 clearance and reporting.
